01

Current · GooseWorks Senior Capstone

Golden Goose Delivery Aircraft

Model in development

Problem / requirements

Design an officially selected twin-boom aircraft configuration for an 11-person AE 171 team, targeting 35 lb MTOW, 15 lb payload, and 18 mi range.

My role

Secretary, configuration advocate, and owner of the five-tab Excel sizing and performance model. I also prepare weekly reports, presentations, and standardized team templates.

Tools and methods

Excel-based mission sizing, battery selection, wing loading, stall speed, tail sizing, lift, drag, power, range, endurance, climb, and graphing.

Design decisions

I advocated for the twin-boom layout because it supports central payload placement, a two-engine layout, aerodynamic shaping flexibility, and carrying capacity.

02

Current · Spartan Racing Formula SAE

Front-Wing CFD Development

Preliminary design

Problem / requirements

Develop the front wing within a student race-car aerodynamics program and establish a repeatable analysis workflow for future designers.

My role

I take primary ownership of front-wing development while coordinating with the official aerodynamics lead.

Tools and methods

SolidWorks, STAR-CCM+, Sabalcore, spreadsheets, and CFD post-processing across multiple front-wing configurations.

Design decisions

I transferred the existing design into the updated vehicle baseline and evaluated 10 CFD iterations, using pressure and velocity fields to inspect likely performance losses.

Observed flow featureLarge high-pressure region + separation behind the flaps and beneath the mainplane

I connected those features to reduced downforce and worsened tire-squirt behavior. No performance improvement is claimed yet; the project remains in preliminary design.

Testing and troubleshooting

Created a 3-page CFD workflow guide and 23-slide onboarding presentation used by at least three designers to standardize CAD preparation, simulation setup, remote execution, convergence monitoring, and post-processing.

Current limitation

Results are diagnostic and comparative at this stage. Demonstrated vehicle-level gains remain future work.

Design and fly Level 1 and Level 2 rockets with controlled stability, uncertain avionics mass, and a reliable recovery system.

My role
